Change only what needs changing

Tutorial

Create your first agent in 3 minutes

The app guides you step by step. Watch the video first, then follow along.

  1. 01
    Pick the job

    Choose from 10 roles such as planning, design, and production. Or start with a blank teammate.

  2. 02
    Choose a name and face

    Name your agent and pick a name color. You can change them later.

  3. 03
    Sign in with the AI you already use

    Sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT account. You can also pick a model that runs on your computer.

  4. 04
    Introduce yourself

    Write what to call you and what you do. Your agent answers with that in mind.

  5. 05
    Connect a work folder

    Pick the folder where your agent creates and saves documents.

  6. 06
    Take the tour

    The app walks you through the agent list, sessions, settings, chat, and the document workspace.

Why Be.Ark

Your agent data is stored on your computer

Unlike existing agent services, core data isn't stored on a server.

Your agent's ontology is stored on your computer, and it grows into an agent that knows you better.

Download your agent data as a .rebe file to keep or copy it anytime.

You don't have to worry about your agent data piling up on a company's servers.

Hand over company documents with confidence

Files and work history are saved on your computer. We don't keep your documents on our servers.

It learns how you work

It remembers your usual templates, your tone, and past decisions. No need to explain every time.

Switch computers and keep going

Keep your agent in a single .rebe file. Restore it from that file on a new computer.

Change AI, keep the memory

Choose a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, or a model running on your computer. The memory lives on your computer, so it carries over.
