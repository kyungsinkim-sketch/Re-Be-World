Supported files

Open the files you already use

No need to convert anything. Your agent reads, edits, and saves the original file.

PPT.pptx
Build decks in your own template

Give it one template and your agent reads its design and structure. It makes proposals and summaries in the same template.

  • Redesign an existing deck with design presets
  • Write in your template, in the same tone and flow
  • The more you use it, the more it builds decks your way
HWP.hwp .hwpx
Fill in official forms as they are

It fills in the forms you receive and edits their tables. Open and save them right in the app.

  • Keeps the form's fonts and table layout
  • Edits the cells and text you choose
  • You can edit directly in the app, too
Word.docx
Change only what needs changing

Depending on your request and context, it drafts a new document or edits an existing one.

  • Keeps formatting and numbering
  • Edits the tables and paragraphs you choose
  • See changes on screen right away
Excel.xlsx
Edit sheets and cells precisely

Your agent reads the sheet and fills in values. Formulas stay, and they recalculate when you open the file.

  • From a single cell to a whole table
  • Keeps formulas and merged cells
  • Creates new files from table data
PDF.pdf
Reads scanned PDFs

It recognizes text in photographed contracts and old records, and uses it in your document work.

  • Reads text PDFs as they are
  • Recognizes text in the first 8 pages of a scan
Markdown.md
Edit notes and minutes by asking

Your agent edits tables and to-dos. See the result on screen right away.

  • Shows tables and to-do lists cleanly
  • Changes only what you asked for
  • You can edit directly in the app, too
