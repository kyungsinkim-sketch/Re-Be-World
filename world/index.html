What it does

From inquiry to reply, in one flow

It's not just a reply writer. It gathers, finds context, drafts, and you review before sending.

  1. 01
    Gathers in one place

    Inquiries that arrive as email, messenger, and DM notifications are gathered in your chat screen.

  2. 02
    Brings up context

    It finds what happened before with the same person in its memory and tells you.

  3. 03
    Drafts in your voice

    It learns your tone from what you've sent. Down to your sentence endings and message length.

  4. 04
    You review and send

    Read the draft and tap approve. Nothing goes out before you do.

Sending

You always press send

One off-tone reply can damage a relationship. So your agent only writes drafts.

Nothing goes out without approval

Email, messages, and texts are sent only when you tap the approval card.

Not happy? Change it

Tell it what to fix and it rewrites the draft.

It shows you what it remembers

You can see and delete the people, memories, and places your agent remembers, right in the app.

You choose its name and personality

It's an agent you named. You set its personality and tone, too.

Why Re-Be World

Your agent data is stored on your phone

Unlike many agent services, your agent's core data isn't stored on a server. It grows into an agent that knows you better from the records kept on your phone. Download your agent data as a .rebe file to keep or copy it anytime.

You don't have to worry about your agent data piling up on a company's servers.

It remembers who you worked with, when, and on what. When the next inquiry comes, it answers from that memory.

  • People Contacts you worked with and your history with them
  • Places Where you shot and met
  • Events Deadlines, schedules, and past collaborations
