    Say "She is a client" and your agent files her in that group.

  2. 02
    Frequent contacts come first

    See at a glance who you talk to and how often.

  3. 03
    Notes on each person

    It remembers tastes and past work together, and uses them next time you talk.

Inbox

From inquiry to reply, in one flow

It's not just a reply writer. It gathers, finds context, drafts, and you review before sending.

  1. 01
    Gathers in one place

    Inquiries that arrive as email, messenger, and DM notifications are gathered in your chat screen.

  2. 02
    Brings up context

    It finds what happened before with the same person in its memory and tells you.

  3. 03
    Drafts in your voice

    It learns your tone from what you've sent. Down to your sentence endings and message length.

  4. 04
    You review and send

    Read the draft and tap approve. Nothing goes out before you do.

  1. 01

    Say "Yes, book it" and it prepares the calendar entry and the reply email together.

  2. 02
    Works with your accounts

    Connect the email and calendar accounts you already use. Nothing to migrate.

  3. 03
    Change it the same way

    Move or cancel a meeting just by asking.
