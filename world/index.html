What sets Re-Be World apart

Your agent data is stored on your phone

Unlike existing agent services, core data isn't stored on a server.

Your agent's ontology is stored on your phone, and it grows into an agent that knows you better.

Download your agent data as a .rebe file to keep or copy it anytime.

You don't have to worry about your agent data piling up on a company's servers.

You own your agent data

Local Vault: your agent's vault lives on your phone

Your sign-in details and everything your agent remembers stay on your phone, not on a server. Local Vault is the name of that design.

You sign in yourself

You sign in to websites yourself, on your phone. Your sign-in details never reach our servers. The agent can neither read nor fill in password and card number fields.

Your Data Planet lives on your phone

The memory, relationships and conversations your agent builds up become your Data Planet, stored on your phone. We do not keep them on our servers.

Only your key opens the .rebe file

Save your Data Planet as one file. Only you know the password and recovery phrase, so we cannot open it either. Restore from that file on a new device.

Change phones without losing your agent

Save your agent to a .rebe file with one button on the Data Planet screen. Use that file to move it to another phone or restore it anytime.

How it works

A draft is waiting when a message arrives

It remembers who wrote and what you talked about before, and drafts a reply in your voice. You just check it and send.

Inbox

From inquiry to reply, in one flow

It's not just a reply writer. It gathers, finds context, drafts, and you review before sending.

  1. 01
    Gathers in one place

    Inquiries that arrive as email, messenger, and DM notifications are gathered in your chat screen.

  2. 02
    Brings up context

    It finds what happened before with the same person in its memory and tells you.

  3. 03
    Drafts in your voice

    It learns your tone from what you've sent. Down to your sentence endings and message length.

  4. 04
    You review and send

    Read the draft and tap approve. Nothing goes out before you do.
